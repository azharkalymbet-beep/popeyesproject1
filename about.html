Our Heritage and History

Founded in 1972 in New Orleans, Louisiana, Popeyes began as a small fast-food restaurant with a big vision: to serve authentic, slow-cooked fried chicken. Over the decades, our passion for rich southern traditions transformed a single location into a globally recognized brand loved by millions of loyal customers around the world.

Our unique culinary philosophy centers on authentic Cajun and Creole recipes. We strictly enforce internal QC standards to ensure every batch meets high expectations. The secret behind our signature crunch lies in our 12-hour marination process and specialized hand-battering techniques.

Pop Culture Fun Fact
